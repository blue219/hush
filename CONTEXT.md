# Hush

Hush is a Muse-assisted meditation experience that presents gradual changes in physiological signals through a living visual field.

## Language

**Session**: One timed meditation, including pauses and any signal gaps, that can be saved and revisited.
_Avoid_: Workout, recording

**Mindprint**: A visual representation of a saved session derived from its available physiological samples; it is not a quality score.
_Avoid_: Calm score, diagnosis

**Soundscape**: The ambient audio selected for a session, currently Mist or Tide.
_Avoid_: Guided meditation, lesson

**Signal gap**: An interval without usable signal data. A gap does not imply a particular mental state.
_Avoid_: Restlessness, poor meditation

**Simulation**: A ten-minute playback of bundled Muse samples used to experience the meditation flow without a live headband.
_Avoid_: Live session
